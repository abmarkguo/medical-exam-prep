# Medical Licensing Exam Preparation Skill (医考通关)

> Authoritative exam preparation system for the Chinese National Medical Licensing Examination (临床执业医师/助理医师资格考试), based on the official 2024 syllabus and PMPH guidance series.

## 1. Overview

This skill helps clinical medical students prepare for China's National Medical Licensing Examination (医师资格考试) — the gateway to becoming a licensed physician. It covers **both stages** of the exam:

- **Practical Skills Exam** (实践技能考试): 100 points, 60 to pass, 2-year validity
- **Medical Comprehensive Written Exam** (医学综合笔试): 600 questions (执业) / 300 questions (助理), 360/180 to pass, computer-based

The national pass rate is approximately 25%, with the written exam being the primary bottleneck (18-22% pass rate). This skill addresses that challenge with four core modules.

## 2. Four Core Modules

### Module 1: Knowledge Lookup (考点速查)

Quickly retrieve exam-relevant knowledge points organized by:

- **Subject**: Basic Medical Sciences, Clinical Medicine, Medical Humanities, Preventive Medicine, TCM Basics, Practice Synthesis
- **Organ System**: Cardiovascular, Respiratory, Digestive, Urinary, Hematologic, Endocrine, Nervous, Musculoskeletal, Reproductive, Pediatric
- **Disease Entity**: Look up any disease for etiology, pathology, clinical features, diagnosis, treatment — with exam-relevant annotations

Every knowledge point cites its authoritative source:
- 《医师资格考试大纲（2024年版）》 — National Health Commission
- 《医师资格考试系列指导用书》 — People's Medical Publishing House (PMPH)
- 《临床诊疗指南》 — Chinese Medical Association
- 10th edition national medical textbooks (人卫第10版教材)

### Module 2: Question Type Practice (题型专项练习)

Generate and explain practice questions for all five official question types:

| Type | Name | Description | Exam Share |
|------|------|-------------|-----------|
| A1 | Single-sentence best choice | Pure knowledge recall, 5 options | ~25% |
| A2 | Case summary best choice | Brief clinical case + diagnosis/treatment | ~25% |
| A3 | Case group best choice | One case, 2-3 related questions | ~20% |
| A4 | Case series best choice | Progressive case, 3-6 questions, evolving info | ~15% |
| B1 | Standard matching | 5 shared options for 2-5 questions | ~15% |

Each practice question includes:
- Complete question stem with clinical scenario
- Five options (A-E)
- Correct answer with detailed rationale
- Key knowledge point mapping
- Common pitfalls and distractor analysis
- Exam strategy tips

### Module 3: Case Analysis Training (病例分析训练)

Generate progressive clinical case scenarios that simulate A3/A4 questions and practical skills case analysis:

- **Case presentation**: Patient demographics, chief complaint, history, physical exam, lab results
- **Progressive disclosure**: New information revealed step by step
- **Multi-angle questioning**: Diagnosis, differential diagnosis, further workup, treatment plan, complications
- **Clinical decision tree**: Each answer leads to the next logical question
- **Scoring rubric**: Detailed point breakdown matching official exam standards

Case categories cover all high-frequency systems:
- Internal Medicine: Cardiology, Pulmonology, Gastroenterology, Nephrology, Endocrinology, Hematology, Rheumatology, Infectious Diseases, Neurology, Psychiatry
- Surgery: General Surgery, Thoracic Surgery, Urology, Orthopedics, Neurosurgery
- Obstetrics & Gynecology
- Pediatrics

### Module 4: Skills Exam Simulation (技能实操模拟)

Simulate the three-station practical skills exam:

**Station 1 — Clinical Thinking (40 points)**
- History Taking (病史采集): Structured template with 15-point scoring rubric
- Case Analysis (病例分析): Complete diagnostic reasoning with 25-point scoring rubric

**Station 2 — Physical Exam & Basic Operations (30 points)**
- Physical Examination: System-by-system checklist (cardiac, pulmonary, abdominal, neurological)
- Basic Operations: Sterile technique, suturing, catheterization, CPR, tracheal intubation, etc.

**Station 3 — Multimedia (30 points)**
- Cardiac/Pulmonary Auscultation: Audio-based diagnosis
- Imaging Interpretation: X-ray, CT, MRI reading
- ECG Interpretation: 12-lead ECG analysis

Each simulation includes:
- Step-by-step operation procedure
- Official scoring criteria
- Common deduction points
- Time management guidance

## 3. Exam Categories Covered

This skill focuses on **Clinical Category** (临床类别):

| Level | Exam | Questions | Pass Score | Duration |
|-------|------|-----------|------------|----------|
| 执业医师 | Medical Comprehensive | 600 (4 units x 150) | 360 | 2h/unit |
| 执业助理医师 | Medical Comprehensive | 300 (2 units x 150) | 180 | 2h/unit |
| Both levels | Practical Skills | 100 (3 stations) | 60 | Per station |

## 4. Written Exam Content Distribution

| Subject Area | Share | Key Disciplines |
|-------------|-------|-----------------|
| Clinical Medicine | ~75% | Internal Medicine (32%), Surgery (23%), OBGYN (9.5%), Pediatrics (10.5%) |
| Basic Medical Sciences | ~15% | Physiology, Biochemistry, Pathology, Pharmacology, Microbiology, Immunology |
| Medical Humanities | ~10% | Medical Ethics, Medical Psychology, Health Regulations (2026 revised) |
| Preventive Medicine | Included | Preventive Medicine, Public Health |
| TCM Basics | New addition | Basic TCM theory (added per 2024 syllabus) |

## 5. Key 2026 Exam Updates

- **Medical Humanities syllabus revised**: Added medical history, Chinese medical traditional ethics, TCM Law
- **Skills exam fully standardized**: All exams at national skills bases, full video recording, unified scoring
- **CBT fully implemented**: All categories computer-based (except minority medicine)
- **Second attempt expanded**: More regions offer clinical/TCM second attempt
- **10th edition textbooks**: Over 20% of test points updated

```
"心血管系统有哪些高频考点"
"卫生法规新增了哪些内容"
```
