## Overview

This project is a comprehensive **medical licensing exam preparation skill** designed for Chinese clinical medical students preparing for the National Medical Licensing Examination (医师资格考试). It is based on the official 2024 syllabus by the National Health Commission (NHC) and the 2026 revised Medical Humanities section, covering both the **Practical Skills Exam** and the **Medical Comprehensive Written Exam**.

The national pass rate is approximately 25%, with the written exam being the primary bottleneck (18-22% pass rate). This skill addresses this challenge with four core modules: **Knowledge Lookup, Question Practice, Case Analysis Training, and Skills Simulation**.

### Key Features

- ✅ **Authoritative Sources**: Every knowledge point cites official NHC syllabus and PMPH textbook references
- ✅ **Dual-Stage Coverage**: Both Practical Skills Exam and Medical Comprehensive Written Exam
- ✅ **Five Question Types**: A1/A2/A3/A4/B1 practice with detailed explanations
- ✅ **Progressive Cases**: Simulated A3/A4 progressive clinical decision-making training
- ✅ **Skills Simulation**: Three-station practical exam full simulation with scoring rubrics
- ✅ **Personalized Plans**: Study plans tailored to exam date and weak areas

## Four Core Modules

### Module 1: Knowledge Lookup
Search exam-relevant knowledge by subject, organ system, or disease entity. Every point is tagged with frequency (high/medium/low) and cites authoritative sources.

### Module 2: Question Type Practice
Generate and practice all five official question types with detailed rationales, exam point mapping, and common pitfall analysis.

### Module 3: Case Analysis Training
Progressive clinical case scenarios simulating A3/A4 questions and practical skills case analysis, covering all high-frequency systems.

### Module 4: Skills Exam Simulation
Full simulation of the three-station practical skills exam with official scoring criteria, step-by-step procedures, and common deduction points.
